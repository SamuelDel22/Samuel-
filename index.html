01 / Learn

One river. Many lives.

The Cauca connects ecosystems, communities, wildlife, agriculture and cities. What happens to the water can matter far beyond one location.

Cauca River · real field photograph · Caucasia, Antioquia
Why it matters

When the land changes, the river feels it.

Illegal mining can involve excavation, vegetation loss, increased sediment and, in some contexts, hazardous substances such as mercury.

1
Land gets disturbedExcavation can reshape soil, riverbanks and habitats.
2
Water carries materialSediment and pollutants can move through connected waterways.
3
Life is affectedPlants, animals and communities can feel the consequences.
Guardian rule: knowledge comes first. You cannot protect what you do not understand.

04 / Mercury and Fish · Cauca River

What does mercury do to fish in the Cauca River?

In the Bajo Cauca region of Antioquia, gold mining has been an important source of mercury pollution. Studies in the Bajo Cauca River have found methylmercury concentrations in fish exposed to mining areas.

Bajo Cauca, Antioquia · gold mining and mercury pollution
What happens in the Cauca

Mercury can enter the food chain.

In aquatic environments, some mercury can be transformed into methylmercury. This form can build up in fish tissues and increase as it moves from one organism to another through the food web.

1
Pollution reaches the riverIn Bajo Cauca, mercury use associated with gold mining can contaminate water, sediments and ecosystems.
2
Fish accumulate itMethylmercury can remain in fish tissues and build up over time.
3
The food chain can amplify the problemFish that eat contaminated organisms can accumulate higher concentrations.
Cauca River fact: Research in the lower basin found significantly higher methylmercury concentrations in fish from areas exposed to gold mining, with Bajo Cauca among the most affected areas.
FOOD CHAIN

How mercury climbs the food chain

  1. Water & sedimentMercury arrives
  2. MicrobesSome becomes methylmercury
  3. Small fishIt builds up in their tissue
  4. Predator fishThey eat other fish: more concentrated
  5. PeopleCan be exposed by eating fish

Bars illustrate the idea (they are not measured values): methylmercury can become more concentrated at each step. This is called biomagnification.

FIELD STUDY

160 fish. One clear signal.

  • 92 from La Mojana (exposed)
  • 38 from the Bajo Cauca (exposed)
  • 30 control fish (Cereté)

Two species were tested: bocachico (Prochilodus magdalenae) and moncholo (Hoplias malabaricus). Methylmercury in muscle was significantly higher in the exposed areas (p < 0.05), and the Bajo Cauca was the most affected.

Read it carefully: this is a sample of fish, not a test of every fish in the river.

Cruz-Esquivel & Marrugo-Negrete · Acta Biológica Colombiana 27(1) ↗
Guardian safety rule: you cannot see, smell or taste methylmercury. Never touch unknown materials or enter polluted water. If you suspect a contaminated site, tell a teacher or adult and report it to the local environmental authority.
Why does it matter? The problem is not only the water: mercury can enter fish and affect biodiversity and the food security of communities that depend on the river.
06 / What is being done

Rules and repair.

Mercury is a global problem. Colombia has laws to cut its use, and people are working to repair damaged land.

  1. 2013
    LAWLaw 1658

    Sets the path to end mercury use in mining and in industry.

  2. 2018
    LAWBan in mining

    In July 2018 the Ministry of Environment announced that the mercury ban in mining was in force, going further than the Minamata Convention requires.

  3. 2023
    TARGETTarget date for industry

    The planned date to end mercury use in industry and other productive processes.

  4. 2024
    REPAIRNative plants in Caucasia

    A press report described 30 hectares of mercury-contaminated land recovered through phytoremediation.

  5. 2026
    ENFORCEMENTOperation against illegal mining

    A press report described 5 mining units destroyed in Caucasia.

Sources: La República (2018), Caracol Radio (2024 and 2026). Laws work when they are enforced, which is why being informed and checking facts matters.

07 / Guardian codex

Words and myths, decoded.

Learn the key terms, then test yourself: myth or fact? Tap a card.

▤

Sediment

Particles of soil and sand carried by water. Digging and bare ground send more sediment into rivers.

Hg

Methylmercury

A form of mercury that can form in water environments and that living things absorb easily.

▲

Bioaccumulation

When a substance builds up inside an organism faster than the organism gets rid of it.

▲▲

Biomagnification

When a substance becomes more concentrated at each step up the food chain.

≋

Watershed

All the land that drains into the same river. What happens upstream can reach downstream.

✿

Phytoremediation

Using plants to help clean or stabilise contaminated soil.
